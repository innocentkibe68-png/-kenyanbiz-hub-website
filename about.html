About Us

Learn how we're helping Kenyan businesses thrive with AI

Our Story

Founded in Nairobi, Kenya, KenyanBiz Hub emerged from a simple vision: to make AI accessible and affordable for every Kenyan business. We noticed that while AI technology was advancing rapidly, many local businesses were being left behind due to complexity, cost, and lack of local support.

Our team of AI enthusiasts, developers, and business strategists came together to bridge this gap. We started by building simple WhatsApp bots for local shops and quickly realized the transformative power of AI when tailored to the Kenyan market.

Today, we're proud to serve businesses across Kenya and beyond, helping them automate operations, enhance customer experiences, and drive growth through practical AI solutions.

Our Mission

To make AI accessible and affordable for every Kenyan business, regardless of size or industry, by providing localized AI solutions that solve real-world problems.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading AI automation agency, known for innovation, reliability, and commitment to empowering businesses through intelligent technology.
